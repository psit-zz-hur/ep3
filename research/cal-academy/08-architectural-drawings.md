## 6. Key dimensions (sourced)
- Site 43,000 m2; **floor area 41,000 m2** (Fondazione) ≈ 410,000 sf (Arup, AR) / 412,000 sf (Structure).
- **Height 19.3 m max; canopy 11.3 m**; 3 storeys + 2 basements (Fondazione). Roof datum = park "pushed up 35 ft".
- Roof 18,302 m2 (2.5 acres), seven hills, slopes to 60°, 6 in soil, 50,000 trays, 1.7M plants, 24 ft gabion grid; 87% vegetated.
- PV cells: 60,000 (Fondazione, Wels) vs 55,000+ (AR); 213,000 kWh/yr.
- **Planetarium dome 90 ft; rainforest dome 90 ft** (Arup). **Piazza skylight 66 x 90 ft** (Structure). "37,000 sf piazza" NOT confirmed.
- Cost $488M; design 2000-05, construction 2005-08, opened 27 Sep 2008; LEED Platinum (double).
- Column grid / floor-to-floor: only derivable from Roof Plan A2.0.006 (grid bubbles) and the 3/32" sections; or from the printed magazines.
- Old campus: 12 buildings + 2 courts, 379,443 sf; African Hall (1931), North American Hall (1916), Steinhart (1923) rebuilt to original volumes.
