- Tank facts (Academy): Philippine Coral Reef = 212,000 gal, 25 ft deep, "hundreds/thousands" of fish; educator guide says ">2,000 reef fish, >100 species"; iNat guide says "~4,000 fish of 100+ species"; MDPI paper census "700+ fish, 53 genera". The exhibit "represents the Anilao / Verde Island Passage region"; corals were fragment-collected by Steinhart biologists at Anilao (2011). The exhibit was recently refreshed with a new dive show and new "Hidden Reef" creatures; three ARMS monitoring structures are on display. Dive shows 11:30 and 1:30 daily; divers do NOT hand-feed fish (MDPI).
- Important status changes: **Claude the albino alligator died 2 Dec 2025 (age 30)** (Academy Swamp Talk page). **Blacktip reef sharks** appear in the 2008-era educator guide and iNat guide but the Academy's Sharks exhibit page (2022-23) says the only live sharks on site are **horn sharks, swell sharks (Steinhart) and pyjama sharks (penguin pool)** — so treat blacktips as historic. ZooChat 2025 says there are no leopard sharks or bat rays in California Coast now. Green anaconda is off-exhibit. Reef Lagoon webcam offline since Jan 2026.

## Confidence codes
**A** = confirmed in exhibit by an Academy source; **V** = reported by visitor/docent source (ZooChat lists, Academy docent blog/Flickr, iNat guide); **P** = plausible (historic, adjacent gallery, or single weak source).

## Hazard / "would eat tank-mates" summary (Philippine Coral Reef)
- Venomous: red lionfish (Venom on the Reef tank), blotched foxface rabbitfish, bluespotted ribbontail ray (Academy: "two extremely venomous spines… can be fatal"), bluespotted stingray, honeycomb whipray, cownose ray (mild), long-spined urchin *Diadema*, fire coral *Millepora*, carpet anemone (strong sting), hammer/bubble coral sweepers.
- Bite risk: zebra moray (crushing teeth, mild), bamboo shark (minor), blacktip reef shark (historic). Unicornfish and surgeonfish carry sharp caudal blades (clown surgeonfish is notably aggressive).
- Predators that would eat tank-mates if "released" into the main tank: lionfish, frogfishes (warty, painted — swallow fish their own size), Indian pompano (jack), harlequin tuskfish and axilspot hogfish (invertebrates), honeycomb whipray (bony fish, crustaceans), harlequin shrimp (eats sea stars). Everything else in the list is a planktivore/grazer/invertebrate-picker.

## Notes on species origin for the "info card"
Yellow tang, goldring bristletooth and bandit angelfish are Hawaiian species (not Philippine natives); wide-band anemonefish is Australian; Tinker's and bandit angelfish are deep-reef species that probably live in the adjacent **Twilight Zone** gallery (which also holds peppermint angelfish *Paracentropyge boylei*, rose-veiled fairy wrasse *Cirrhilabrus finifenmaa*, candy-cane coral, flashlight fish and new-to-science anthias/damsels — not included below). A separate **Caribbean reef tank** in the same gallery holds queen angelfish *Holacanthus ciliaris* and cherubfish *Centropyge argi* (Academy educator guide) — also excluded from the Philippine JSON.
