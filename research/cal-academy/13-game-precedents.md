## What to borrow
- Abzû / Journey: no HUD, movement is the content, ride a creature, meditation points. Our plaque-hover and ride-a-ray.
- Subnautica: depth as the organising structure and a scanner that builds a databank. Our three water layers and the species log.
- Beyond Blue / Endless Ocean / Alba / Pokémon Snap: real species, info cards, photography or spotting as the collection verb. Our 99-species roster; consider a camera mode.
- Outer Wilds: discovery is the only progression and the log is a museum. Our dive loop should be solvable by curiosity alone.
- SOMA / BioShock: drowned architecture read as place. Our African Hall and planetarium sphere.
- Animal Crossing museum / Two Point Museum: the museum itself as the artefact visitors love. Our plaques and sealed tanks viewed from the water.
