## 8. Gaussian-splatting suitability judgement
Best (rich static texture, diffuse light, loopable):
1. African Hall architecture and dioramas (shoot diorama glass obliquely; skip the penguin tank end).
2. Living Roof deck + hills (static, high-frequency texture; shoot overcast to avoid PV speculars).
3. Main entrance / T. rex lobby (loop around the mount; mask glass walls).
4. Piazza floor and perimeter (treat canopy as a separate layer).
5. Osher Rainforest ramp levels 1-3 (foliage and rockwork; lens fogging at 85 F / 75% RH; mask the dome glass).
6. Foucault pendulum alcove.
7. Giants of Land and Sea / Color of Life gallery architecture (mask screens).
8. Exterior terraces and facade colonnade.
9. Academy Cafe / Terrace pavilion.
10. Project Lab / Naturalist Center (cluttered static specimens).

Hard / avoid: aquarium tanks (refraction, fish, dark), Amazon tunnel (curved acrylic), Swamp (water + moving animal), planetarium interior (black; do the exterior shell), dome glass / canopy / curtain walls (model as geometry), mirrors and screens, Shake House, midday crowds (mask with SAM / Mask2Former).

Capture order hint: Living Roof -> T. rex lobby -> African Hall -> rainforest ramp (lens-fog break) -> Piazza perimeter -> facades/terraces.
